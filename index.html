Copytrade Wallet Score privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · applies to version 1.23.0 and later

Copytrade Wallet Score analyzes public Solana blockchain data in your browser. The developer runs no server and collects nothing: no account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Everything you enter or the extension works out stays in your own browser, except the lookups it sends to the data services listed below to do its job.

What the extension handles

  • Your Helius API key or keys. You paste these in Settings. They are saved in your browser and sent only to Helius, as part of each data request, so Helius can answer it.
  • Wallet and token addresses. The addresses you type or paste, and the ones the extension finds while scanning a token's traders or holders. These are public blockchain addresses, not personal accounts with names attached.
  • Analysis results. Scores, recommended filters, trade histories pulled from the blockchain, token names and prices, saved Holders checks, and your History list.
  • Your settings. Scoring rules, filters and display choices.

The extension does not ask for or read your name, email, browsing history, passwords, private keys, seed phrases or wallet balances you haven't looked up. It never connects to or signs with a crypto wallet and cannot move funds.

Services it contacts

To look up blockchain data the extension sends requests straight from your browser to these services. Each request contains only what that lookup needs.

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
Helius (mainnet.helius-rpc.com)Your API key, and the wallet or token address being looked upTransaction history, token details and holders
GeckoTerminal (api.geckoterminal.com)Token addressesToday's token prices and price history
Binance.US (api.binance.us)Nothing about you; a request for SOL's price historyConverting SOL amounts to dollars

These services handle requests under their own privacy policies. The developer has no access to what you send them.

GMGN pages

On gmgn.ai the extension reads the address of the page you are on and the token links on it, so it can show a score badge and labels for wallets you have analyzed. It does not click, type, submit or change anything on GMGN, and it does not send anything from the page anywhere. You can turn this off in Settings → Analysis → Show Wallet Score on GMGN pages.

Storage and deletion

All data is kept in your browser's extension storage on your device. It is not synced to the developer and not shared with anyone.

  • Trades older than 31 days are removed automatically.
  • History → Clear history removes saved wallets, scores and Holders checks.
  • Settings → Storage shows what is saved and lets you clear each kind of data.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it saved.

Permissions

  • Side panel: shows the extension next to the page you are browsing.
  • Storage and unlimited storage: keep your settings, keys and saved wallet trade history on your device so reopening a wallet is free.
  • Access to the four sites above: the three data services, plus gmgn.ai for the page labels and for reading which wallet or token page you have open.

Limited use

The use of information received by Copytrade Wallet Score adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's wallet analysis. It is never sold, never transferred to third parties except the data services above as needed for that purpose, never used for advertising, and never used to decide creditworthiness or for lending.

Other terms

  • The extension is an analysis tool. Its scores and filters are estimates from past trades, not financial advice.
  • It is not affiliated with GMGN, Helius, GeckoTerminal or Binance.US.
  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the Support tab on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
